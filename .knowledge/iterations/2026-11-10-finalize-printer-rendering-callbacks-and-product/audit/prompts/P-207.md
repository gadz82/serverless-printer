# Task

Implement the two-role Terraform contract: a generated/provided caller role for publishing jobs,
and a generated/provided worker execution role for consuming jobs and publishing to delivery
channels.

# Constraints

- **Caller:** `caller_role_mode` default `create`; generated role trusts
  `caller_trusted_principal_arns` (default deployer account root) and has `sqs:SendMessage` only on
  the jobs queue; output `caller_role_arn`. `existing` requires `caller_role_arn`, adds it to the
  queue senders, and creates/modifies no IAM. Cross-account callers are allowed via their trust.
- **Worker:** `worker_role_mode` default `create`; generated role is the current least-privilege
  policy plus channel IAM (`lambda:InvokeFunction`, `events:PutEvents`, existing `sqs:SendMessage`)
  scoped to `callback_target_arns`; same-account only; `existing` requires `worker_role_arn` and
  creates/modifies no IAM; resolved ARN feeds Lambda, KMS and the smoke queue.
- **Bootstrap:** add `additional_worker_role_arns` to `iam:PassRole`; add EC2/VPC management
  permissions for `vpc_mode` create/lookup. Keep the deploy role trust unchanged.
- **Outputs:** `caller_role_arn`, `worker_role_arn`, `caller_role_created`, `worker_role_created`.
- Do not merge caller/worker permissions. No shared role.
- Validate ARNs, same-account worker, forbidden ARNs in create mode, and required ARNs in existing.
- Update tfvars/README with the role matrix and provided-role responsibilities.
