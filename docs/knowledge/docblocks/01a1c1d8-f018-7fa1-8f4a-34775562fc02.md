# validateJob

## Behavior

Compiles the job schema once with Ajv 2020 and validates every inbound job before the worker maps it to domain types.

## Why

The queue is the only entry point; rejecting malformed jobs at the boundary keeps the domain and the public contract aligned.

## Invariants

- The schema is the source of truth and validation is strict.
- Validation errors are formatted without leaking payload values.

## Failure modes

A schema drift or an invalid fixture rejects the job; callers receive INVALID_MESSAGE when a readable failure callback exists.

## History

R-002, S-003, S-004, ADR-001 — initial Phase 1 contract boundary.
